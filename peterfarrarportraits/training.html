Your Photography Training

Learn Photography Your Way

Photography is much easier to understand when you have someone beside you who can explain it in practical terms and answer the questions that matter to you.

Peter Farrar offers personalised 1 to 1 photography training in Basingstoke and Hampshire, designed around your current ability, your camera, and what you want to achieve.

Whether you have just bought your first camera, want to move beyond automatic mode, or are an experienced photographer looking to improve specific areas of your photography, the training is built around you.

Training Built Around You

There is no fixed classroom syllabus and no assumption that everyone learns in the same way. You learn what you need to know, at a pace that suits you.

What You Can Learn

Photography Training That Fits You

From Beginner to Advanced

Training can start with the absolute basics and progress as far as you want to take your photography.

For beginners, this can include understanding your camera, choosing the right settings, focusing correctly and learning how shutter speed, aperture and ISO work together.

For photographers with some experience, training can concentrate on moving into manual exposure, improving composition, understanding light and getting more consistent results.

For more advanced photographers, sessions can focus on professional techniques, portrait photography, lighting, advanced camera control, workflow, creative techniques and developing your own photographic style.

What You Can Learn

Your training session can be tailored to the areas where you want the most help.

  • Understanding your camera and its controls and how to use it
  • Learn about exposure with Aperture, Shutter and ISO combined
  • Understanding Metering Modes
  • Understanding depth of field
  • Understand Focusing Modes
  • Understanding lenses and focal lengths
  • Composition and Rules
  • Working with natural light and with Flash off or on Camera
  • White balance and colour
  • Image sharpness and avoiding common technical problems
  • Developing your photographic eye and style
  • Working with subjects and directing people
  • Building confidence with your camera
  • Improving your photographic workflow
  • Editing and post processing
  • Preparing images for print and online use
  • Developing a consistent photographic style
  • Understanding professional photography
  • Building a Photography Business
  • Marketing Photography
  • Pricing Photography

Training Built Around Your Camera

You do not need to buy a particular camera or equipment before training.

Bring the camera and lenses you already own and we can work with them.

One of the advantages of 1 to 1 training is that you learn how to use your own equipment properly rather than being taught using equipment that you do not own.

We can work through your camera menus, settings and controls and concentrate on the features that will actually improve your photography.

Practical Photography Training

Photography is best learned by taking photographs.

Where appropriate, training can take place with the camera in your hands rather than simply sitting in front of a computer or listening to me and a presentation, the combination works.

You will be able to take photographs, change settings, see the results and understand why the image changed.

This practical approach helps turn technical information into knowledge that you can actually use.

Learn From Experience

To help you become more confident, more knowledgeable and more capable with your camera.

Your Training Session

Before your session, we can discuss what you want to achieve and identify the areas where you need the most help.

The session can then be structured around your objectives.

You may want to spend the entire session learning your camera.

You may want to improve your understanding of exposure.

Or you may simply have a long list of photography questions that you have never been able to get answered.

That is exactly where 1 to 1 training works well.

No Jargon. No Pressure.

Most people never learn the fundamentals needed for constantly good results every time.

Photography can sometimes appear unnecessarily complicated.

There are hundreds of camera settings, technical terms and conflicting opinions about the right way to take a photograph.

The objective is not to overwhelm you with technical information.

It is to explain what matters, demonstrate it in practice and help you understand how to make your own photographic decisions.

You should leave your session understanding your camera better than when you arrived.
